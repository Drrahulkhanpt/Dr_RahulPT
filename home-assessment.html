WHY HOME ASSESSMENT?

Your environment is part of your recovery.

Physiotherapy is not only about what happens inside a clinic. Your home, daily routine, mobility challenges and support system can directly influence your recovery.

A home assessment allows the physiotherapist to understand your actual functional environment and design rehabilitation around the way you live.

आपकी recovery केवल clinic में नहीं होती। आपके घर का environment, daily activities और mobility challenges भी rehabilitation का महत्वपूर्ण हिस्सा हैं।

WHAT WE ASSESS

We assess the person, not just the problem.

Every home session begins with understanding your current functional status, symptoms, movement and rehabilitation needs.

01

Symptoms & Pain

Understanding your symptoms, pain behaviour, aggravating factors and how they affect your daily activities.

02

Mobility & Movement

Assessment of walking, transfers, joint movement and movement strategies relevant to your condition.

03

Strength & Function

Functional strength, balance, endurance and activity tolerance are considered according to your individual needs.

04

Home Environment

We look at practical factors such as stairs, walking space, bed and chair transfers and other environmental challenges.

05

Daily Activities

We identify which everyday activities are difficult and where physiotherapy can make the greatest functional difference.

06

Rehabilitation Goals

Your priorities and realistic functional goals help guide the rehabilitation plan from the beginning.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

More than a treatment session.

The goal is to leave you with a clearer understanding of your condition and a practical direction for rehabilitation.

01 / ASSESS

Clinical Assessment

A structured physiotherapy assessment based on your symptoms, movement, function and rehabilitation requirements.

02 / UNDERSTAND

Functional Understanding

We identify the major functional limitations affecting your mobility and day-to-day activities.

03 / PLAN

Rehabilitation Direction

You receive a clear direction for treatment, exercise and progression based on your individual assessment.

04 / GUIDE

Home-Based Guidance

Practical advice for exercises, mobility, positioning and safe activity within your home environment.
