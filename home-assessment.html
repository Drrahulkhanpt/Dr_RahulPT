THE ASSESSMENT

What Happens During the Visit?

The assessment focuses on understanding the patient's current functional status and deciding the appropriate rehabilitation direction.

01 Clinical History

Understand the diagnosis, symptoms and relevant history.

02 Physical Assessment

Assess relevant movement, strength, mobility and function.

03 Functional Evaluation

Look at how the condition affects everyday activities.

04 Rehabilitation Planning

Identify priorities and appropriate rehabilitation goals.

05 Exercise Guidance

Provide suitable exercise and progression guidance.
