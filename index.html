MY CLINICAL APPROACH

Rehabilitation Starts With the Right Assessment

Every patient is different. The same diagnosis does not always mean the same rehabilitation approach.

Sirf exercise karna rehabilitation nahi hai. Sahi patient ke liye, sahi exercise, sahi intensity aur sahi progression choose karna important hai.

01

Assess

Understand the patient's condition, functional limitations and goals.

02

Identify

Identify the key factors limiting function and rehabilitation progress.

03

Plan

Develop a structured rehabilitation strategy based on assessment findings.

04

Prescribe

Select appropriate exercises, intensity and progression.

05

Progress

Gradually challenge the patient according to response and functional goals.

06

Reassess

Measure progress and modify the rehabilitation plan when required.
