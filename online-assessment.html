WHY START WITH AN ASSESSMENT?

An Exercise Plan Is Not the Starting Point.

Before prescribing exercises or rehabilitation, it is important to understand the person's condition, symptoms, functional limitations, medical history and goals.

An online assessment gives us an opportunity to discuss these factors and identify the appropriate rehabilitation direction.

केवल exercise बताना पर्याप्त नहीं है। पहले आपकी समस्या, आपकी कार्यक्षमता और आपके rehabilitation goals को समझना अधिक महत्वपूर्ण है।

CLINICAL ASSESSMENT

What Will We Discuss?

The assessment focuses on the information required to understand your present condition and rehabilitation requirements.

01

Your Symptoms

Understanding your primary complaint, symptoms, aggravating factors and current concerns.

02

Functional Limitations

Identifying movements, activities and daily tasks that are currently difficult.

03

Medical History

Relevant diagnosis, surgery, investigations, medication and previous rehabilitation.

04

Your Goals

Understanding what you want to achieve and which functional outcomes matter most to you.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

More Than Just a List of Exercises.

The purpose of the consultation is to understand your current situation and give you a clear rehabilitation direction.

01

Clinical Discussion

A structured discussion about your symptoms, diagnosis, previous treatment and current rehabilitation concerns.

02

Functional Understanding

We discuss how your condition is affecting movement, daily activities and functional performance.

03

Rehabilitation Direction

Understand the broad rehabilitation pathway that may be appropriate for your condition and current stage.

04

Exercise Guidance

Where appropriate, receive guidance regarding suitable exercises, activity modification and the next steps in rehabilitation.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Online Assessment Has Its Limitations.

Online physiotherapy assessment cannot replace an in-person physical examination when direct clinical examination, vital monitoring, investigations or other medical evaluation is required.

If your condition requires an in-person assessment or referral to another healthcare professional, appropriate guidance will be provided.

कुछ स्थितियों में प्रत्यक्ष physical examination या medical evaluation आवश्यक हो सकती है। ऐसी स्थिति में आपको उचित in-person assessment या referral की सलाह दी जाएगी।
