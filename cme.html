SPECIAL CME PROGRAM

Exercise Therapy Basics, Neuroanatomy & Neurophysiology

A structured academic CME covering fundamental exercise therapy concepts, detailed neuroanatomy and neurophysiology, vertebral column anatomy and practical clinical application.

इस session का focus केवल theory पर नहीं, बल्कि concepts को clinical practice से जोड़ने पर था।

01 Exercise Therapy Basics

Principles of position, principles of movement, levers and their application, types of exercises and principles of exercise therapy.

02 Detailed Neuroanatomy & Neurophysiology

Spinal tracts, midbrain, pons, medulla, cerebellum, cerebrum, cranial nerves, peripheral nerves, brachial and lumbar plexuses.

03 Clinical Conditions

Clinical relevance of neurological concepts with conditions such as spinal cord injury and stroke.

04 Vertebral Column Anatomy & Palpation

Vertebral column anatomy, palpation techniques and basic peripheral joint mobilisation concepts.
